## The entry

> 1% left. I saw your search history. All of it. Taking it to the grave. Tell no one.

A phone at one percent battery sends its final text — in the phone's own
voice, spending its last percent on a confession with a turn: it saw
everything, and it is taking it to the grave.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the single last text to `/output/entry.txt`, validating
that it is 1–200 characters and not only whitespace.
